The 200-item rating test reports correlations on its own sample; on all 1,049 rated items they were 0.82, 0.28, 0.44 and 0.77 (Section~\ref{sec:method}). Its rule's go branch was to test a deployable open model; once the starting kit showed that submissions may call an API during scoring, I used Claude itself instead, a change not fixed in advance. Two earlier choices of the final entry had rules too. Before uploading V3 I decided that it would be final if it ran, whatever its score. Once V4 passed the rating tests, I replaced that rule (a change not fixed in advance) with one making V4 final if the billing page showed its ratings had been requested during scoring, which it did (\$9.49). The V4-or-V5 rule above then superseded it. Two short trial runs of the rating test, with an unreliable reply parser, were discarded before the recorded run.

\paragraph{Scenarios of the trust tests.}
In the rating-trust test, half-noisy ratings keep each item's rating with probability 1/2 and otherwise take the rating of a random other item of the benchmark. Section~\ref{sec:results} reports the regrets of both trust tests.

\paragraph{Choices without a rule.}
The baselines, V1's prior scale of 0.5 and a stress test of V1 not reported here (held-out benchmarks made 1 or 2 logits easier or harder) had no rule fixed in advance. Neither had V3's switch to a scale of 0, which I made from formative feedback although it was worse offline ($+0.00300$ Brier ALC, SE 0.00047, in the default setting and $+0.00459$, SE 0.00055, in the hidden-sized setting with $K=1$).

\paragraph{The first run of the V5 test.}
This run stored the guess trust in the attribute that held V4's rating trust. Claude Code, the coding assistant I used (Section~\ref{sec:repro}), found the bug by tracing one pair after guess trust 0.25 had scored worse than both 0 and 0.5 at budget 31. It was fixed without changing the rule, and I report only the rerun. The invalid run, which tested a different model, had also chosen a guess trust of 0.75 and passed; it is not in the repository.
